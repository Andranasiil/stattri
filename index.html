Where the work happens

Every engagement starts with the same question: what decision does this data need to support? The service that follows depends on the answer.

Clinical research

Statistical analysis plans, sample size and power calculations, and analysis support from pre-clinical work through PMA submission. Certified in Clinical Research Conduct and Management (UC Berkeley Extension).

Tutoring & training

One-on-one instruction in statistical methodology and software — SAS, SQL, Excel, R, and Python — for graduate students and teams building in-house capability.

Manuscript preparation

Research design consultation, analysis, and writing support to get a study from data to a submission-ready manuscript.

Clinical research

Rigor from day one

Medical research carries a higher bar for documentation and defensibility at every stage. Analysis plans, methodology, and reporting are built to hold up under review — from an IRB committee to a journal referee.
